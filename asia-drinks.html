Masala Chai

Simmer ginger and cardamom in water.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Black tea, milk, water, ginger, cardamom, sugar.

Simple preparation

Simmer ginger and cardamom in water. Add tea, then milk and sugar; bring to a gentle simmer and strain.

Matcha Latte

Whisk sifted matcha with a little warm water until smooth.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Matcha powder, warm water, milk.

Simple preparation

Whisk sifted matcha with a little warm water until smooth. Top with warmed, frothed milk.

Mango Lassi

Ingredients

Ripe mango, plain yogurt, milk, cardamom.
