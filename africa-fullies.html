West Africa Vegetarian

Vegetable Jollof Rice

Blend tomatoes, peppers and onion.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Rice, tomato, red pepper, onion, vegetable stock, thyme.

Simple preparation

Blend tomatoes, peppers and onion. Cook the mixture down, add washed rice and vegetable stock, then cover and cook until tender.

Ethiopia Non-vegetarian

Doro Wat

Slowly cook chopped onion, then add butter, ginger, garlic and berbere.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Chicken, onion, berbere spice, butter, garlic, ginger.

Simple preparation

Slowly cook chopped onion, then add butter, ginger, garlic and berbere. Add chicken and water and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.
