Kumara Soup

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Kumara (sweet potato), onion, vegetable stock, coconut milk.

Simple preparation

Sauté onion, add chopped kumara and vegetable stock, and simmer until soft. Blend and stir in coconut milk.

Meat Pie

Cook beef and onion, stir in flour and stock and simmer until thick.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Pastry, minced beef, onion, flour, beef stock.

Simple preparation

Cook beef and onion, stir in flour and stock and simmer until thick. Cool, enclose in pastry and bake until golden and thoroughly heated.
