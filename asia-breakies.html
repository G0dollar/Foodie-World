India Vegetarian

Poha

Rinse flattened rice briefly.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Flattened rice, onion, mustard seeds, turmeric, peanuts, lemon.

Simple preparation

Rinse flattened rice briefly. Temper mustard seeds, cook onion and peanuts, then gently fold in rice and turmeric. Finish with lemon.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.

India Vegetarian

Idli

Grease idli moulds, fill with prepared fermented batter, and steam until set.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Fermented rice-and-urad-dal batter, water, oil for greasing.

Simple preparation

Grease idli moulds, fill with prepared fermented batter, and steam until set. Serve with coconut chutney or sambar.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.

03 / BREAKIES

Photo: jetalone · CC BY 2.0 · Source

Japan Non-vegetarian

Tamagoyaki

Beat eggs with soy sauce and a little sugar.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Eggs, soy sauce, sugar, oil.

Simple preparation

Beat eggs with soy sauce and a little sugar. Cook thin layers in an oiled pan, rolling each layer into the next; cook through and slice.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.
