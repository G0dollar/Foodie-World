Poha

Rinse flattened rice briefly.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Flattened rice, onion, mustard seeds, turmeric, peanuts, lemon.

Simple preparation

Rinse flattened rice briefly. Temper mustard seeds, cook onion and peanuts, then gently fold in rice and turmeric. Finish with lemon.
