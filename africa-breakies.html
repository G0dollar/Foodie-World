East Africa Vegetarian

Uji

Mix flour with cold water to form a slurry.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Millet or sorghum flour, water, milk or sugar if desired.

Simple preparation

Mix flour with cold water to form a slurry. Stir into simmering water and cook, stirring, until smooth and fully cooked.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.

Photo: Orrling · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Source

East Africa Vegetarian

Mandazi

Mix an egg-free dough with the flour, coconut milk, sugar, baking powder and cardamom.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Flour, coconut milk, sugar, baking powder, cardamom, oil.

Simple preparation

Mix an egg-free dough with the flour, coconut milk, sugar, baking powder and cardamom. Rest, roll, cut and fry carefully until cooked and golden.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.
