Chana Masala

Cook onion, ginger and garlic with spices.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Cooked chickpeas, onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, cumin, garam masala.

Simple preparation

Cook onion, ginger and garlic with spices. Add tomatoes, then cooked chickpeas and water; simmer until the sauce thickens.

Vegetable Sushi Rolls

Ingredients

Sushi rice, nori, rice vinegar, cucumber, carrot, avocado.

Photo: Delhifoodwalks · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Source

South Asia Non-vegetarian

Chicken Biryani

Marinate chicken in yogurt and spices.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Chicken, basmati rice, yogurt, onion, ginger, garlic, biryani spices.

Simple preparation

Marinate chicken in yogurt and spices. Cook the chicken, layer with partially cooked rice and browned onions, and steam covered until chicken and rice are fully cooked.

A simplified overview; ingredients and methods vary by household.
