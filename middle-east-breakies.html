Manakish with Zaatar

Shape dough into small flatbreads.

Ingredients & preparation

Ingredients

Flatbread dough, zaatar, olive oil.

Simple preparation

Shape dough into small flatbreads. Mix zaatar with olive oil, spread over the dough and bake until cooked and lightly golden.
